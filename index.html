A note from the principal

I play with rocks. Professionally.

When people ask what I do, I tell them I am a geologist. That is true. The more complete answer is that I remove what is covering the interesting part, I write it down, and I do not blush.

I took my master’s at UC Santa Barbara in marine science and geology. I have been a geologist at Parsons, a staff scientist at Conestoga-Rovers, and a person who made environmental consultants at SWCA look organized. I have also done time in the El Dorado County Office of Education, which is how I learned that adults require more supervision than children.

By day I am Project Control Coordinator at Novaterra Biological Consulting. I keep the work on budget, on schedule, and inside the permit. By night I apply the same standards to a slightly less regulatory audience.

I have been stripping formations since 2003. The tools have changed. The cleavage has not.
